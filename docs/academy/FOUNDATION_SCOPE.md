# Academy Phase 1 foundation scope

Status: owner approved for implementation on 2026-07-16.

## Audience and curriculum decision

The first academy foundation targets Grade 2 learners in teacher-supervised classroom or school pilots in the Philippines. Content aligns to the DepEd Revised K–10 MATATAG Mathematics Curriculum, Grade 2 Quarter 1, Number and Algebra.

English mathematical terminology is the initial language. Domain and content identifiers must remain locale-neutral so instructions, explanations, audio, and contextual problems can later use Filipino or another learner-familiar language without changing historical evidence identity.

## Mathematical scope

The version-1 taxonomy is limited to:

1. count, recognize, read, and represent whole numbers to 1,000;
2. count by 2s, 5s, 10s, 20s, 50s, and 100s within 1,000;
3. compare and order whole numbers to 1,000;
4. identify hundreds, tens, and ones in three-digit numbers;
5. determine the place and value of a digit;
6. illustrate addition using movement and counting up on a number line;
7. add using expanded form;
8. add with sums to 1,000, with and without regrouping;
9. understand the zero, commutative, and associative properties of addition through age-appropriate representations.

The current Jigsaw Math activity emits structured evidence only for reviewed addition items that map to this scope. The taxonomy includes the other approved Quarter 1 skills so future reviewed content can use stable identifiers, but Phase 1 does not add another minigame.

## Activities

- **Jigsaw Math:** structured arithmetic practice with correct-answer puzzle progression. Phase 1 tags reviewed addition items and does not use answer speed as a status signal.
- **Number Line Expedition:** approved as a later candidate but explicitly not authorized in Phase 1.

## Intended use and prohibited claims

This application is a supplementary formative-practice tool. Personal summaries help a learner and supervising adult see what has been practiced recently and where review may be useful.

It is not a diagnostic instrument, grading system, placement test, summative assessment, or replacement for teacher instruction. Phase 1 must not claim formal mastery, diagnose a misconception or learner condition, compare children, or describe a child as weak, failed, bad at mathematics, below ability, or a slow learner.

## Deliberate exclusions

Phase 1 excludes the complete Grade 2 annual curriculum; multiplication; division; fractions; money; geometry; perimeter; time; formal teacher mastery predictions; competition; student accounts; cloud synchronization; remote classrooms; public/social features; and any backend or teacher web dashboard.

No learner name, email, date of birth, precise location, advertising identifier, contact, photo, microphone/camera data, free-form child content, analytics event, or crash-reporting payload is added.

## Pilot decision threshold

The pilot asks whether the activity is usable, curriculum-aligned, educationally meaningful, and able to produce credible learning evidence. It does not claim effectiveness before an appropriately designed evaluation. All taxonomy mappings and evidence-policy thresholds remain product hypotheses requiring educator review and validation.
